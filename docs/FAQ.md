# PackPerks — Frequently Asked Questions

Plain-language answers to the questions people ask most. Two parts:

1. **For customers** — the people bringing their own cup.
2. **For venue staff** — the people working the counter (no PackPerks dashboard needed).

Keep it simple. If something here does not answer the question, the support form is linked at the bottom of each section.

---

## For customers

### How does it work, in one line?
Bring your own cup, scan the little QR code on the counter, and collect cups. Once you have enough, you get real cashback. No app to download, no points that expire into thin air.

### Do I need to download an app or make an account?
No. It runs in your phone's browser. You can add your email so your cups are saved to you and follow you to a new phone, but you can start collecting straight away without one.

### I scanned the code but nothing happened / it says it is being reviewed. Did I lose the cup?
No, nothing is lost. To keep things fair there is a small daily limit per venue, so an extra scan can be held for a quick check before it is added. If it was a genuine visit it will show up shortly. If a scan ever fails, just try once more.

### How do I actually get the cashback?
Three steps:
1. Collect enough cups for the reward you want.
2. Buy that item at the venue and keep the printed receipt.
3. Open PackPerks, upload a photo of the receipt, and we send your cashback by a payout link once it is checked.

### My receipt was not approved. Why, and what now?
The screen tells you which rule it missed, for example the reward item was not on the receipt, the receipt was too old, or the photo was a screenshot rather than the printed receipt. Your cups stay safe. Fix the point it mentions and upload a new receipt using the "Upload a different receipt" button.

### How long until the money arrives?
Usually a few days after your receipt is approved. You get an email the moment it is approved with the link to collect it.

### Do my cups expire?
No. Your cups stay in your account. Collect at your own pace.

### I changed phones or cleared my browser. Are my cups gone?
They are safe if you saved your email. Open PackPerks, choose to sign in, enter that email, and your cups come back to the new device.

### Can I use cups I collected at one location somewhere else?
Yes, as long as it is the same venue or brand. Cups you collect stay with that venue, and you can spend them at any of its locations.

### Do you sell my data?
No. We use only what is needed to run the programme: your cup balance, your claims, and your email if you added one. No ads, no third-party trackers. You can view, export, or delete your data at any time from the account page.

### The QR code will not scan.
Hold steady in good light and fill the frame with the code. If your camera is blocked, allow camera access or scan the counter code with your phone's normal camera instead. If it still will not read, let a staff member know so they can check the code.

### Something is wrong and none of this helps.
There is a **Contact support** link inside the app on your account page. Tell us what happened and your email, and a real person will reply.

## For venue staff

You do **not** need a PackPerks login or any dashboard. Customers do everything on their own phones. You cannot break anything, and you are never expected to handle anyone's money or data. This section is just so you know what to say.

### What is PackPerks, simply?
A reward for customers who bring their own reusable cup. They scan a QR code on the counter, collect cups, and later get a little cashback. It is good for them and good for cutting single-use waste.

### Do I need to do anything at the till?
No extra steps. Serve the drink in the customer's own cup as usual. They scan the counter code themselves. You do not add or remove cups, and you do not scan anything for them.

### A customer says their cup was not added. What do I say?
Reassure them nothing is lost. Ask them to try scanning once more. Explain there is a small daily limit, so an extra scan can be held for a quick review and will appear shortly. You cannot add cups by hand, and that is normal — it keeps the programme fair for everyone.

### A customer is asking where their cashback or payout is.
Payouts are handled automatically by PackPerks after their receipt is checked, usually within a few days, and they get an email with a link. It is not something the counter handles. Point them to the app, or to the **Contact support** link on their account page.

### A customer's receipt was rejected and they are upset. Can I override it?
No, and you are not expected to — this is not on you. They re-upload a new photo themselves in the app. It usually helps to gently explain the three things a receipt needs: it must be the **printed receipt** (not a screenshot), it must clearly **show the reward item**, and it must be **recent**. If they meet those and it still fails, they can reach support from the app.

### The QR code on the counter is damaged, missing, or looks wrong.
Do not try to recreate it yourself. Let your manager know, or use the support form so PackPerks can send a fresh one.

### Can I see customer accounts, balances, or personal details?
No. Staff have no access to any customer data, and that is by design. There is nothing you can look up or change, so you never have to worry about privacy here.

### A customer wants to complain or has an idea.
Take it kindly — most people just want to feel heard. For anything you cannot answer, point them to the **Contact support** link in the app, or pass it to your manager. You are not expected to resolve technical issues at the counter.

### Where do I get help for myself?
Use the vendor support form (your manager has the link) or ask your manager. Describe what you saw and, if it is about a specific customer, roughly when it happened. You will not be blamed for a technical hiccup — reporting it is exactly the right thing to do.
