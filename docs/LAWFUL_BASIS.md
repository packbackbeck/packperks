## Scope and controller

PackPerks is a reusable-cup loyalty and cashback service. Customers earn per-store cup balances by scanning cups and submitting receipts, and are paid cashback rewards by bank transfer. This register records the lawful basis under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation 2016/679) for each distinct processing purpose in the PackPerks MVP.

PackPerks is the sole **controller** for all processing below. Partner stores receive **only aggregate statistics** through an admin-only dashboard; they never receive user-level histories, emails, IBANs, receipt images or behavioural profiles, and are therefore not joint controllers of the personal data in this register.

**Architecture context relevant to lawful basis.** The system is a React/Vite single-page app on Vercel with no traditional backend; all personal data lives in Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions) hosted in the EU region (`eu-west-1`). Customers are anonymous by default — a random `device_id` stored in `localStorage` links an anonymous customer to their balance and is **essential**, not analytics. Optional email sign-in (Supabase magic-link/OTP) saves the account across devices. Receipt images are sent to the Anthropic Claude API in the **United States** for verification; PackPerks uses the standard Anthropic API where inputs are not used for training and are not retained beyond the request (zero-retention default), with a DPA and an EU–US Data Privacy Framework / Standard Contractual Clauses transfer basis recorded as an action.

**UAE note (general).** For users in the United Arab Emirates, the corresponding basis under Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL) is noted per row. The PDPL's default is **consent** (Art. 4), with limited exceptions that broadly track the GDPR grounds — performance of a contract to which the data subject is a party, protection of the data subject's interests, controller's legal obligations, and the controller's legitimate interests where they do not prejudice the data subject. Cross-border transfers to the US (Anthropic) rely on PDPL Arts. 22–23 (adequate safeguards / contractual clauses / data-subject consent) in addition to the GDPR transfer basis.

## Notes on reliance

- **Contract (Art. 6(1)(b))** covers the parts of the service a customer directly asks PackPerks to perform: tracking cups, honouring rewards, restoring an account by email, and paying out by IBAN.
- **Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))** covers receipt AI verification, fraud prevention / rate-limiting, and admin/security logging; each is supported by a documented necessity-and-balancing assessment and uses only data already needed for the service.
- **Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c))** covers the 7-year NL accounting retention of claim records and the fulfilment of data-subject rights.
- **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a))** covers optional email provision and, separately, non-essential behavioural analytics, which are written only after "Accept all" and are fully withdrawable.
- **Marketing:** none is carried out. Any future marketing would require separate, freely given consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) and is out of scope of this register until introduced.

## Purpose limitation — fraud & security signals

The rate-limit counters and fraud signals PackPerks generates — including **duplicate-receipt detection**, **receipt-vs-cup-return timestamp checks**, and **per-claim / per-user rate limits** — are processed under **legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))** **strictly for fraud prevention and security**. These signals are **firewalled** from any marketing, advertising, or behavioural-targeting / re-engagement use: they may not be repurposed to profile customers, to build audiences, or to drive offers. This is a **policy constraint on how the data may be used**, not a technical change — it does **not disable any existing feature**; the same fraud and rate-limiting controls continue to operate exactly as before. Introducing any new purpose for these signals (in particular any marketing or profiling use) would require a fresh lawful-basis assessment and, where consent-based, separate consent.

Changes to purposes, data, bases or retention must be reviewed and re-dated by the **PackPerks Privacy Owner** before adoption.
