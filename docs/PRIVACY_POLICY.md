# PackPerks Privacy Statement

**Version July 2026**

*Nederlandse versie hieronder — Dutch version below.*

When you use PackPerks, your personal data is processed. PackPerks is the web application from PackBack that allows you to register returned reusable cups and collect and view cashback or other rewards. With this privacy statement, PackBack informs you about the way we process your personal data. We explain why we process personal data about you, how we do this, and which rights you have. Our contact details can be found at the bottom of this privacy statement.

PackBack is convinced that protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy is of great importance. Personal data is handled with the greatest possible care. We consider it important that the processing of personal data takes place in a manner that complies with existing safeguards for the protection of privacy. In doing so, we follow the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and related applicable laws and regulations, as well as this privacy statement.

## 1. Definitions

**User** — Anyone who visits or uses the PackPerks web application.

**PackPerks** — The web application of PackBack, available at perks.packback.app, through which Users can register returned reusable cups and collect and view cashback or other rewards.

**Service** — The cashback and rewards program offered by PackBack in cooperation with participating hospitality partners, aimed at encouraging the use and return of reusable packaging.

**PackBack** — The private limited company PackBack Network B.V., located at Saturnusstraat 95 1.06, 2516AG The Hague, The Netherlands, registered in the trade register of the Chamber of Commerce under number 84383860.

**Hospitality Partner** — The hospitality business affiliated with PackBack through which the Service is offered.

## 2. What personal data does PackBack process?

Personal data is data that can directly or indirectly identify you. Within PackPerks, we mainly process the following categories of personal data:

- **Email address.** This is optional and is used to log in via a one time code, and to save your account, collected cups and cashback so you can access these from different phones and browsers. If you give your consent, we also use your email address for marketing communication.
- **Receipt (photo).** When you claim cashback, you upload a photo of your receipt. We automatically read data from it, such as the date, total amount and purchased items, to check whether your claim is valid.
- **Usage data**, including the number of returned cups, your cashback balance, your submitted claims, and whether you donate your cups instead of taking cashback.
- **Device id.** A random code stored in your browser so we can recognise a returning device without you having to log in. You can reset this code yourself.
- **Technical data**, including your IP address and information about the device and browser you use to access PackPerks. This is collected, among other things, because your browser automatically fetches files from third party servers while using PackPerks, for example for the store finder map or product images.
- **Other personal data** that you share with us yourself, for example in a message to us.

Cashback payouts are facilitated by Tikkie, a service of ABN AMRO Bank N.V.

## 3. How does PackBack obtain this personal data?

PackBack receives your personal data because you provide it to us yourself, in particular through PackPerks, for example by entering your email address. You are not obliged to provide this data. If you choose not to provide certain data, such as your email address, we will of course not process it, although this may mean that, for example, you cannot access your history from another device.

Without your receipt (or your Tikkie details), we cannot assess your claim or pay out your cashback. The purposes of these processing activities are described in more detail below.

We do not process special categories of personal data, such as your national identification number or medical data. You should not provide these to us either. Should you accidentally provide such data, we will delete it as soon as possible after receipt.

PackBack uses AI technology, provided by our processor Anthropic, to automatically assess whether a receipt you submit is valid for granting cashback. If you disagree with the outcome of this assessment, you can always contact us at info@packback.network for an additional human review of your claim.

You can only use PackPerks if you are older than 16 years. PackBack is not liable for the consequences of processing data of persons under the age of 16 without the consent of their parent or guardian. If we become aware of this, we will take steps to delete this data. If you suspect that your child has provided us with personal data without your consent, please email info@packback.network.

PackBack does not retain your personal data for longer than necessary to perform the Service. In principle, this means that we retain your data for as long as you maintain an account with PackPerks. If you delete your account, we will also delete your personal data, except for data we are legally required to retain for longer.

## 4. On what basis and for what purposes does PackBack process your personal data?

### 4.1 Processing of your data when you visit PackPerks without creating an account

When you visit PackPerks without creating an account, we process the technical data mentioned above and your device id for the following purposes:

- Making PackPerks available.
- Safeguarding the functioning and security of PackPerks.
- Recognising your device, so your collected cups remain visible without you having to log in.
- Carrying out analyses on the use of PackPerks.
- Adapting PackPerks to your needs.

We may process your personal data for these purposes on the basis of our legitimate interest. In that case, our legitimate interest in having PackPerks function optimally outweighs your privacy interest.

### Cookies and similar technologies

PackPerks uses functional cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for the web application to work, including your device id. If you select Accept all in our cookie notice, we additionally collect our own, first party usage statistics, for example which screens you visit. These statistics are stored in our own database within the EU. We do not use third party cookies or trackers, such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel. If you select Decline, your collected cups remain visible, but we do not record any usage statistics.

In addition, while you use PackPerks your browser automatically fetches files from other companies' servers, for example map imagery for the store finder and product images shown for rewards. This shares your IP address with those companies at the moment the map or an image is loaded. See section 5 for an overview of these parties.

### External links

PackPerks may contain links to websites that are not operated by PackBack. We are not responsible for the practices of other websites linked to or from PackPerks. Our privacy statement does not apply once you follow a link to a third party website. We recommend that you review the privacy statements on those websites.

### 4.2 Processing of your data when you create an account and use PackPerks

When you create an account and use PackPerks, we process your personal data for the following purposes:

- Making PackPerks available.
- Facilitating the Service.
- Safeguarding the functioning and security of PackPerks.
- Carrying out analyses on the use of PackPerks.
- Adapting PackPerks to your needs.
- Establishing your identity as a User.
- Keeping track of the number of cups you have returned and the cashback accumulated as a result.
- Assessing receipts you submit for cashback, including with the help of AI technology.
- Paying out cashback via Tikkie, or processing your choice to donate cups instead of taking cashback.
- Storing your account and history, so you can view these from different phones and browsers.
- Providing information about your account within PackPerks.

We may process your personal data for these purposes on the basis of the performance of the agreement you enter into with PackBack by creating an account.

### Newsletter and marketing

If you give your consent, we may use your email address to send you newsletters and inform you about our products, the Service and offers. If you no longer wish to receive these, you will find an unsubscribe option in every email, or you can report this free of charge via info@packback.network.

### Withdrawing your consent and unsubscribing from the newsletter

Withdrawing your consent only applies to the future and does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. If you withdraw your consent or unsubscribe, we will delete your personal data from our systems within four weeks, unless we are required by law to retain it for longer, or retaining it for longer is necessary to carry out our activities or to settle the agreement.

## 5. Access to and security of your personal data, and disclosure to third parties

Only PackBack employees who need access to your personal data to carry out their work for the Service have direct access to your personal data in PackBack's systems.

Providing your receipt and e-mail address is necessary to assess and pay out a cashback claim. Providing your email address is optional and only needed if you want to save your account and history or stay informed about news. You can leave other fields blank if this data is not necessary for the purposes mentioned above. If you choose to provide more data than necessary, you do so on the basis of consent, which you can always withdraw later via info@packback.network.

### Security of your personal data

PackBack handles your personal data with care and ensures appropriate organisational and technical security measures to protect your data against unlawful access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Information exchanged, collected and analysed through PackPerks is transmitted in encrypted form.

If, despite these measures, a security incident occurs that is likely to have adverse consequences for your privacy, we will inform you of the incident as soon as possible, along with the measures we are taking to limit the consequences and prevent recurrence.

Various parties are involved within the Service, including the Hospitality Partner where you bring your cups. PackBack may share aggregated and, where possible, anonymised data about the number of reused cups with the Hospitality Partner, so that it can gain insight into the use of reusable packaging within its own locations.

In addition, PackBack engages the following third parties to provide PackPerks:

This runs almost the entire Service: our database, login functionality, storage of files such as receipts and cup photos, and the server functions that process claims and payouts. We will only share your personal data with third parties if this is necessary to perform the Service, for analytical purposes, or to comply with a legal obligation. Where PackBack works with third parties who process personal data, we enter into a data processing agreement with them, which sets out that they will only process your data in accordance with this privacy statement.

### Transfers outside the EEA

To automatically assess your receipt, we share the receipt and the data derived from it with Anthropic in the United States. Our hosting provider, the map imagery of the store finder, and some product images may also result in your IP address reaching parties outside the European Economic Area (EEA). For these transfers, we ensure appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or participation in the EU US Data Privacy Framework. Your other personal data, including your email address and usage data, remains stored within the EEA, with Supabase in Ireland.

- **E-mailadres.** Dit is optioneel en gebruik je om in te loggen via een eenmalige code, en om je account, gespaarde bekers en cashback te bewaren zodat je deze kan terugvinden vanaf verschillende telefoons en browsers. ## 6. Jouw rechten ten aanzien van de verwerking van je persoonsgegevens

Je hebt de volgende rechten met betrekking tot de verwerking van je persoonsgegevens:

- Je kan verzoeken om informatie en inzage in de persoonsgegevens die wij van je verwerken. Dit houdt in dat je kan opvragen welke persoonsgegevens er van je zijn geregistreerd en voor welke doeleinden die persoonsgegevens worden gebruikt.
- Je kan bezwaar maken tegen de verwerking van je persoonsgegevens, bijvoorbeeld als je van mening bent dat het gebruik van je persoonsgegevens niet noodzakelijk is voor de uitvoering van onze activiteiten of het voldoen aan een wettelijke verplichting.
- Je kan ons verzoeken je persoonsgegevens te laten wijzigen of corrigeren, of de verwerking van je persoonsgegevens te beperken.
- Je kan ons vragen om je persoonsgegevens te verwijderen uit onze systemen.
- Je kan ons vragen om je persoonsgegevens aan een andere partij over te dragen.
- Ben je het niet eens met een automatische beoordeling van je bonnetje, dan kan je vragen om een aanvullende menselijke beoordeling van je claim.

Je ontvangt in beginsel binnen vier weken na ontvangst van je verzoek een reactie. Wij zullen aan je verzoek voldoen, tenzij wij een dwingend gerechtvaardigd belang of wettelijke verplichting hebben om de persoonsgegevens niet te verwijderen, dat zwaarder weegt dan je privacybelang, of wanneer het voldoen aan je verzoek anderszins niet wettelijk verplicht is. Hebben wij de persoonsgegevens verwijderd, dan kunnen wij om technische redenen niet altijd alle kopieen onmiddellijk uit onze systemen en back-up systemen verwijderen. Is voldaan aan een verzoek om persoonsgegevens te verbeteren, aan te vullen of te verwijderen, dan informeren wij ook derden aan wie deze persoonsgegevens zijn verstrekt over de aangebrachte wijzigingen.

Verzoeken en andere berichten over de uitoefening van deze rechten kan je schriftelijk indienen via de contactgegevens hieronder. Zorg ervoor dat je verzoek of bezwaar is voorzien van een motivatie, je naam en e-mailadres. Wij kunnen je vragen om je nader te identificeren.

Daarnaast staat het je altijd vrij een klacht in te dienen bij de relevante privacytoezichthouder. Dat is de Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, bereikbaar via www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

## 8. Wijziging privacyverklaring

Wij kunnen deze privacyverklaring van tijd tot tijd aanpassen of updaten. Aanpassingen treden in werking op het moment dat ze zijn gepubliceerd binnen PackPerks. Wij raden je aan deze privacyverklaring regelmatig te raadplegen, zodat je op de hoogte bent van eventuele wijzigingen. Zijn aanpassingen voor jou van significant belang, dan informeren wij je hier altijd apart over, bijvoorbeeld per e-mail.
