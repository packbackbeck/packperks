# PackPerks — Vendor Onboarding Form (Bring-Your-Own cup)

**Welcome!** This short form gives us everything we need to set your café live in the PackPerks app — your branding, your locations on the map, your rewards, and how we verify and pay out cashback. Most of it is copy-paste from what you already have.

**How to fill it in**
- Fill the blanks (`______`) and tick the boxes (`[ ]` → `[x]`).
- Amounts: use **€** (Netherlands) or **AED** (UAE) — whichever is your currency.
- Send back this file plus your **logo** and one **sample receipt photo**.
- For the reward numbers, use the separate **PackPerks Reward Pricing Guide** (the "four numbers + formula" sheet). This form just captures the results.

*Legend: **(required)** must be filled to go live · **(optional)** improves the experience · 🖼️ = send a file.*
