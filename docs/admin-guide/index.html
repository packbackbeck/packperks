Start here

Getting started

The admin console lives at /admin on the PackPerks site. Sign in with your @packback.network account. Only that email domain can create an admin account, so if you cannot register, ask a teammate to invite you.

One store at a time

Almost everything you see is scoped to a single active store. The active store shows at the top left (next to the PackPerks mark) and again at the bottom left of the sidebar. To work on a different store, switch it first (see Switching stores and groups).

Roles and what they can do

Your role decides which pages you can open and whether the controls inside them are editable. If a page is missing from your sidebar, or a button looks greyed out, your role does not have access.

RoleWhat it can do
OwnerFull control, including the Organizations list, groups and regions.
AdminFull control of stores, rewards, design, claims and settings.
ManagerDay to day work: rewards, claims, design, users and reports. Limited on org level settings.
CheckerReview focused: claims, cup scans and users. Editing controls are disabled.

Orientation

The interface

Every page shares the same frame: a top bar, a left sidebar and the main content area. Learn these three and the rest of the dashboard is easy to move around.

Top bar

  • PackPerks mark and store name on the far left tell you which store you are editing.
  • Search the dashboard (also opens with Cmd/Ctrl + K) jumps to any page or setting by name.
  • Timezone shows the clock the data is displayed in.
  • Settings, Help and Version history are the three small icons. Help opens the support page; Version history lets you roll back a previous publish.
  • Preview opens the customer app with your current unpublished draft.
  • Publish pushes your staged changes live. See Draft, Preview and Publish.

Left sidebar

  • The page list is your main navigation. The set of pages changes slightly per store type; a Bring Your Own store shows BYO QR Codes, for example.
  • The Cashback and Refund rates block near the bottom is a shortcut; clicking it opens Org settings.
  • The store switcher and your profile sit at the very bottom.

Navigation

Switching stores and groups

Click the store name at the bottom left to open the switcher. Standalone stores are listed flat. Stores that belong to a group are nested under the group name.

  • Click any store to switch to it. The whole dashboard reloads scoped to that store.
  • The gear on a store opens that store's settings.
  • The gear on a group opens the Organizations page, where group members are managed.
  • Add organisation starts the new store wizard. Manage organisations opens the full Organizations list.

Grouped stores such as the Bring Your Own vendors share a single customer balance, so a cup earned at one member is redeemable at the others.

How changes go live

Draft, Preview and Publish

There is no separate staging website. Instead PackPerks uses a draft for each store. When you change design, copy or settings, those edits save to a draft for the active store. Customers keep seeing the last published version until you publish.

  1. Make your edits on App Design, Rewards & Offers or Org settings. They save to the draft automatically or with a Save changes button.
  2. Press Preview in the top bar to open the customer app with your draft, so you can check it before anyone else sees it.
  3. When it looks right, press Publish to push the draft live.

Publish is immediate and public. It affects the live customer app for that store right away. If something looks wrong after publishing, use Version history (top bar) to roll back to an earlier snapshot.

Daily work

Claims

Claims are cashback and refund requests from customers, each with a receipt photo. This is where you approve or reject them.

Reading the list

  • The counts at the top read Pending Approved Rejected. Filters and a search box sit under them.
  • Validation is an automatic first check on the receipt, for example Passed, Flagged, not a receipt or wrong item. Treat it as a hint, not a decision.
  • Amount is the cashback value. Tikkie status tracks the payout once approved. Decided by shows who reviewed it.
  • Use the Table and Review toggle at the top right to switch between the list and a focused one at a time review mode.

The two everyday tasks

Approve a claim and pay the customer

Most frequent
  1. Open Claims, then click Review at the top right to switch from the table to the review layout. It selects the first pending claim for you.
  2. The review panel opens on the right: the receipt photo, the AI check, the claimant and the payout amount. Click the photo to enlarge it and confirm it is a genuine receipt that matches the reward.
  3. When you are satisfied, press Approve & Pay.
  4. In the confirm box, check that every receipt rule reads Yes, add a note if you want, then press Approve & pay. A payout link is created (Tikkie in the Netherlands) and the customer is notified.

Reject a claim

Frequent
  1. In the same review panel, press Reject instead of Approve.
  2. In the confirm box, mark each rule that failed as No. The rules you mark No are the exact reasons the customer sees on their rejected screen, so be accurate. You must mark at least one No and add a short reason.
  3. Press Reject claim. The customer keeps their cups, sees why it failed, and can upload a new receipt. No payout is sent.

Approve & Pay creates a real payout. Check the receipt matches the reward and the amount is right before you confirm. A rejection is recoverable; a payout is not. If the confirm box warns that you are overriding the AI, look once more before committing.

Daily work

Users

Everyone who has opened this store's app. The header counts split them into two kinds.

  • The Show visitors toggle (top right) hides or shows people who have not earned a cup yet.
  • Search by name, email or the short ID (first 8 characters).
  • Columns cover type, marketing opt in, device, current cups, lifetime cups, joined and last active.
  • Click any customer to open their detail panel on the right (balance, claims, activity). Drag the divider to resize it. Opening a customer from Cup Scans lands you here with them already selected.

If a customer used the app on two devices and ended up with two accounts, an account merge can join them. Large or frequent merges are held for review to protect balances.

Setup

Rewards & Offers

The catalog of rewards customers redeem their cups for. The list is on the left; the editor for the selected reward is on the right.

  • Add Reward creates one from scratch. Import CSV and Download template handle bulk changes.
  • Rearrange order lets you drag rewards into the order customers see.
  • The editor has Setup (status, release window, name, description, price and cups needed, image), plus Promotion and Content tabs.
  • A reward's status can be Draft, Scheduled, Live, Paused, Expired or Archived. A release window can flip it live and expired on set dates automatically.
  • Set featured stars a reward so it stands out in the app.

Add a new reward

  1. Press Add Reward at the top right. To add many at once, use Import CSV with the downloadable template instead.
  2. In the editor, set the Status (keep it Draft while you work, set it Live when ready), then fill in the name, description, price and cups needed, and add a photo. A release window can flip it live and expired on set dates for you.
  3. Your edits save to the draft. Press Publish in the top bar to put the reward in front of customers.

Archive retires a reward from the menu. It is kept for reporting rather than deleted, but customers can no longer claim it. Reward changes save to the draft, so Publish to make them live.

Setup

App Design

Controls how the customer app looks and reads for this store. Each store has its own design; switching the active store loads its own draft.

  • Tabs across the top: Colors, Copy, Sections, Guide stories and Smart import.
  • The palette sets the brand colours (primary, accent, deep accent), the two surface layers and the text colours. Contrast ratios are shown so text stays readable.
  • Save changes stores your edits to the draft; Revert discards them.

Use the live preview and the top bar Preview to check a change, then Publish when you are happy. Nothing here is public until you publish.

Bring Your Own

BYO QR Codes

This page belongs to Bring Your Own stores. It holds the counter QR that customers scan to earn a cup, and the review queue for scans that go over the daily limit.

  • Counter QR: print it and stand it on the counter. Each scan adds one cup to the customer's balance.
  • Location: pick the location so scans are attributed to the right address. Each location has its own QR. Download PNG saves the poster.
  • Auto-credit limit: how many scans one customer can auto-collect per 24 hours before extra scans need review. Change it and press Save limit.
  • Review queue: when a customer goes over the limit, the extra cup lands here under Pending. Approve to credit it, or deny to reject.

Print the counter QR for a location

  1. Choose the Location from the dropdown so scans are credited to the right address.
  2. Press Download PNG, then print it and stand it on the counter. Every scan adds one cup to the customer's balance.

Change the daily auto-credit limit

  1. In Auto-credit limit, set how many scans one customer can auto collect per 24 hours.
  2. Press Save limit. Scans above the limit are not lost; they wait in the queue below for you to approve or deny.

Records and review

Cup Scans

A log of every cup return scan for this store, with its photo and location. It is also the review queue for Bring Your Own scans: when a customer goes over the store's daily auto-credit limit, the extra scan is held here for you to approve or reject.

  • Held scans float to the top with a purple accent and an Approve / Reject action. A "N awaiting review" chip in the header jumps straight to them.
  • The Table / Review toggle (top right) opens a resizable split with a detail panel on the right. Drag the divider to set the width.
  • Click a customer in any row to jump to the Users page with that person already open.

Review a held cup scan

Bring Your Own
  1. Open Cup Scans. Held scans sit at the top with Approve / Reject right in the row, or click the awaiting review chip to filter to just them.
  2. To see the full picture first, switch to Review and select the row. The panel explains why it was held and who it belongs to (click the customer to open them on Users).
  3. Press Approve & credit to add the cup to the customer's balance, or Reject to credit nothing.

Approving credits a real cup. Only approve over-limit scans you believe are genuine. The daily auto-credit limit itself is set per store on the BYO QR Codes page.

Growth

Future Vendors

Places that are not live yet but appear as coming soon pins on the customer Stores map. They hint to customers that more locations are on the way.

Data and email

Reports and emails

Two jobs live here. The top of the page builds and exports data on demand. Further down are three email tools that send information out on a schedule or the moment something happens.

  • Dataset: Users, Claims, Cup Scans or Activity. Tick the columns you want, pick a time period, choose a format (CSV, Excel, JSON or PDF).
  • Export CSV, Copy and Print / PDF are at the top right.

Export a data file

  1. Pick a dataset, tick the columns you want, set a time period and a format, then press Export. The rows update as a live preview so you can check before you download.

The three email tools

  • Automated reports: emails a scheduled data export, for example a weekly valid claims summary per store for vendor direct debits.
  • Weekly digest: a designed email of the metrics you pick (new users, cups collected, cashback paid, CO₂ avoided and more), sent every week.
  • Notification center: an instant email the moment something happens. You choose the events: new claim, new account, cup scanned, BYO cup request or an account merge held for review.

Each tool has its own Send to address, a Scope (this store, or the whole group) and a Send test now button so you can check the email before turning it on.

Turn on instant email alerts

  1. In Notification center, switch it On, then tick the events you want to hear about (new claim, new account, cup scanned, BYO cup request, account merge).
  2. Set the Send alerts to address and the Scope, press Send test now to check it arrives, then Save notifications.

Diagnostics

System Health

A go or no-go dashboard for the technical return flow: QR generation, scanning, rewarding and dashboard tracking. It is where you confirm the mechanics are working for a store.

  • The banner at the top gives an overall Go or No-go verdict.
  • Each card (scan success, correct organization page, cup count, event accuracy, duplicate handling, critical errors, user stuck rate and more) reports a percentage against Go, Conditional and No-go bands.
  • Use Inspect rows on a card to see the exact scans behind the number.

A metric can read Not measurable or N/A when there is not enough data yet. That is expected for a new or quiet store, not an error.

Diagnostics

User Behaviour

The behavioural view of the same audience: how visitors turn into users, where people drop off, and how they move through the app. Use it to understand the funnel rather than individual accounts.

Configuration

Org settings

Everything for one store in a single place: profile, locations, team, payout rates, app copy and policies. Open it from the store gear in the switcher, or by clicking the rates block in the sidebar.

  • Tabs: Profile, Locations, Team, Activity, Payout rates, App copy, Cup rules, Feature flags.
  • Profile (via Edit): URL slug, brand name, KVK and BTW, address, country, contact details and brand colour.
  • Locations: Add location, or Edit and Delete an existing one.
  • Team members: Invite teammate by email and set their role.

Add a location

  1. On the Locations card, press + Add location, fill in the address, and save. Each location gets its own BYO QR so scans are attributed correctly.
  2. Use Edit to change a location later. Delete removes it and its QR, so use it with care.

Invite a teammate

  1. On the Team members card, press + Invite teammate, enter their email, choose a role, and send. They receive an invite and get that role on their first sign in.

Country drives payouts. It sets the currency and the payout method (the Netherlands uses Tikkie). The wrong country means no payout link for customers. Also treat Delete location with care; it removes the location and its own QR.

Configuration

Organizations & Groups

The PackPerks staff view of every client brand. This is where you add stores, switch between them, bundle them into groups, and take one offline. The group gear in the switcher and the Manage organisations link both open this page.

  • Per store: Open settings, Switch to, Duplicate and Soft delete.
  • Add organisation starts the new store wizard.
  • Groups (such as Bring Your Own) bundle stores that share a customer balance. Regions at the bottom handle multi regional setups.

Soft delete hides a store from the switcher and the customer app. The good news: data is preserved and the store can be restored later. It is safer than a hard delete, but customers lose access straight away, so confirm you have the right store first.

When you are stuck

Getting help

Open the help page from the question mark icon in the top bar. It has quick links to resources and three forms that reach the PackBack team.

Where does support email go

Each form opens your own mail client with a prefilled message, then sends to the right inbox. Every message includes a short context footer with your role, organisation, browser and the page you were on, so the team can triage faster.
